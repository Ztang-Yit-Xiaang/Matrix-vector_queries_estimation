\section{Reproducibility and provenance}\label{sec:record}
The accompanying source package maps each theorem and empirical finding to
the detailed project records~\cite{project2026}. It includes original
schematics, numerical figure sources, and a separate development history.
The three quantitative plots use the validated records; only internal study
labels were removed from two plot subtitles. No plotted value or interval
was changed.

Some experimental outputs required protocol-based recovery after smoke
tests overwrote untracked files. The recovered orientation and Hessian
results reproduce the recorded protocols, but unavailable original hashes
prevent a claim of byte-identical restoration. The companion recovery audit
documents this limitation.

Uncertainty follows the replication unit. Certification repetitions are
clustered within paths, and paths can share orientations. The reported
bootstrap intervals are conditional on the frozen population, not universal
performance guarantees. The exploratory orientation and Hessian tables do
not receive new confidence intervals in this manuscript.

The two conceptual figures are original, programmatically drawn schematics,
not experimental measurements. AI assistance supported editorial revision,
illustration code, and proof auditing. Scientific claims and authorship
metadata require author verification before submission.
