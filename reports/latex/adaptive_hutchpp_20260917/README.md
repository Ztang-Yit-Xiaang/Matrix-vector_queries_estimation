# Adaptive Hutch++ — LaTeX manuscript draft

Open `main.tex`. Upload this folder, or the accompanying ZIP, to a LaTeX editor and select `main.tex` as the main document. The sources, bibliography, and all six figures are included. No PDF is included or newly generated.

## Writing model and scope

The organizational reference is Meyer, Musco, Musco, and Woodruff, **Hutch++: Optimal Stochastic Trace Estimation**, [arXiv:2010.09649v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09649). This draft follows its theory-led progression: problem and contributions, preliminaries, numbered results with proofs, experimental validation, references, and supporting appendices. It does not reproduce the paper's prose, claim its optimality result for our methods, or attempt to replicate a publisher's exact class file.

The draft retains the detailed September 17 report rather than reducing it to a short paper. Its fifteen report-local result blocks, spectral corollary, six empirical findings, historical qualifications, and six figures are retained. Splitting the structured-energy and graph arguments produces **17 formal result environments and 17 proof environments**. Existing proof-note numbering remains unchanged; cross-references inside this manuscript are generated by LaTeX.

The substantive claim is an analysis of query allocation, randomized subspace capture, and certification limits. It is **not** a uniformly better allocator, a universal +1 oversampling law, or a complete small-budget confidence theorem.

## Organization

1. Introduction and contributions.
2. Preliminaries, accounting, unbiasedness, and exact conditional risk.
3. Rank-aware marginal allocation.
4. Subspace capture and an indistinguishable pilot transcript.
5. Direct risk estimation, simultaneous acceptance, and paid-query cost.
6. Explicit confidence constructions and their limits.
7. Six experimental findings with the frozen evidence and limitations.
8. Discussion and the unresolved paired-confidence problem.
9. Appendices: corrected moments, scale boundary, research history, and provenance.

`SOURCE_MAP.md` maps the original result labels and empirical findings to the new source sections. `assembly_manifest.json` records original source locations and hashes. `provenance/` contains archival Markdown evidence notes; their original relative links refer to the research repository and are not a standalone navigable website.

## Compilation and validation

Use pdfLaTeX with BibTeX. In this folder, a normal user-requested build is:

```sh
pdflatex main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex main.tex
pdflatex main.tex
```

For this delivery, compilation was instead checked with `pdflatex -draftmode`, BibTeX, and resolving passes in a temporary directory. Draft mode checks typesetting, images, citations, and cross-references without producing a PDF. The final log has no undefined references, undefined citations, or overfull boxes. This is a compilation check, not a rendered-page visual review.

Twenty focused mathematical regressions and five validated-figure checks pass. The full estimator suite was not rerun for this editorial task. Historical artifacts and the original Markdown report are protected by checksum checks. `validation_manifest.json` records the checks and source hashes. Zero new matrix-vector queries and zero bootstrap resamples were used.

## Editorial decisions and remaining author review

- The byline is deliberately neutral. Confirm the author name(s), institutional affiliation(s), and advisor attribution before external circulation.
- The report's accidental sample-variance exponent `^{,2}` is typeset correctly as `^{\,2}` here. The archived original is unchanged.
- Use `S` for range sketches, `g` for certification/final probes, `q` for attempted width, and `r` for accepted rank. The knee is denoted `k` with its correspondence to the earlier `r_star` notation stated in the preliminaries.
- Standard identities are not presented as novelty. Imported results have bibliographic citations; repository-specific empirical claims remain explicitly tied to the frozen local evidence.
- The three conceptual diagrams are AI-generated explanatory artwork. The three quantitative figures are unchanged data-rendered originals. They must not be interchanged with generated numerical-looking illustrations.
- Confirm submission venue, page limit, byline, and preferred bibliography format before a submission-focused revision. This is a detailed research draft, not a submission-ready claim of peer review.
